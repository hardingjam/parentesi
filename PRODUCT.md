## Platform

## Users

Two audiences, confirmed 2026-10-31, neither ranked above the other:

- **Event clients:** private hosts and companies booking a pop-up dinner, tailor-made catering, a cooking workshop or food consulting. They come to judge whether parentesi fits their occasion, then get in touch.
- **Guests and followers:** people who have eaten at a parentesi dinner or follow @parentesi.berlin / @planatia on Instagram, and come to find out what the project is.

Venues/collaborators and consulting businesses were offered as audiences and not chosen. They may still arrive, but the site isn't designed around them.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio site of ( parentesi ), Pietro Platania's itinerant, plant-based food project, based in Berlin. It shows what parentesi is (the manifesto), what it offers (services), who cooks (Pietro's CV), what the food is like (sample menu, photographs), the sibling music project Planatia, and how to reach Pietro.

Success: an event client gets in touch, or a guest or follower leaves understanding the project. The site doesn't take bookings or payments; contact happens by email, phone or Instagram.

## Positioning

The owner confirmed all four differentiators (2026-10-31), in this order:

1. **Circular and conscious:** circular, plant-based cooking and conscious consumption are the principle behind everything.
2. **Itinerant one-offs:** there is no fixed restaurant. Each dinner is a _parenthesis_, a suspended moment in a different place.
3. **Food and music as one:** dinners are sensory, art-adjacent events. "Tastes like sound = Planatia. Sounds like taste = ( parentesi )."
4. **Michelin pedigree:** Le Cordon Bleu Paris; Le Bristol\*\*\*; Il Luogo di Aimo e Nadia\*\*; Joia\*; Vun\*\*; Chez Panisse; founder of Banco Natural Food, Rome.

The claim is the combination. A neighbouring supper club might have one of these, not all four.

## Product Principles

1. **Getting in touch is the one action.** For an event client, every path ends at a direct line to Pietro: email, phone or Instagram.
2. **The principle leads and the plate proves it.** Circular, conscious cooking comes first. The CV and the photographs are the evidence, not the headline.
3. **Every claim is real.** The CV, menu, services and contacts appear as the owner gave them, and placeholders stay visibly provisional.
4. **Music is a sibling, not an add-on.** Planatia belongs to the same idea, even while its section stays a teaser.
